1.2 Network Topology Overview

  • CAN: VPN (Cisco ISR) • ASA firewall (stateful filtering) • IPS (يراقب ويمنع) • L3 switches (ACL، DHCP snooping، DAI، IP source guard) • L2 switches (port security، 802.1X) • ESA/WSA • AAA server • Hosts (antivirus، HIPS).
  • SOHO: consumer-grade wireless router فيه firewall، port security، WPA2، antivirus.
  • WAN: بتغطي مساحة كبيرة، ASA + VPN tunnels على الـ edge.
  • Data center: physical security: outside perimeter (حراس، أسوار، بوابات، كاميرات) و inside perimeter (كاميرات، motion detectors، security traps، biometrics، exit sensors) + HVAC و UPS.
  • Cloud/VM: Hyperjacking، Instant On Activation، Antivirus Storms.
  • Borderless/BYOD: MDM = data encryption، PIN enforcement، data wipe، DLP، jailbreak/root detection.

2.1 Who is Attacking Our Network?

  • Terms: threat، vulnerability، attack surface، exploit، risk. إدارة الـ risk: acceptance، avoidance، reduction (الأكتر استخداماً)، transfer.
  • Hackers: white hat / gray hat / black hat.
  • Threat actors: Script kiddies (1990s، مش للربح) • Vulnerability brokers (gray hat، مكافآت) • Hacktivists (احتجاج سياسي) • Cybercriminals (black hat، فلوس) • State-sponsored (أسرار حكومية وتجسس).
  • Cybersecurity tasks، IOC (دليل إن هجوم حصل) و IOA (استراتيجية المهاجم)، CISA/AIS، NCSAM (أكتوبر)، ENISA.

Define/discuss: Threat, Vulnerability, Attack surface, Exploit, Risk (also asked as a matching question)

Threat: potential danger to an asset (data/network). Vulnerability: weakness in a system or its design that a threat can exploit. Attack surface: total sum of vulnerabilities in a system accessible to an attacker. Exploit: mechanism used to leverage a vulnerability (remote or local). Risk: likelihood that a threat will exploit a vulnerability and cause an undesirable consequence.

Final Dec 2024 (M.Sc.)Final 23/1/2025 (عام)Final Jan 2025 (كريديت)Final Dec 2025 (كريديت)Midterm Nov 2025 (عام)

Four common ways to manage risk. Describe each.

Risk acceptance: cost of management > cost of risk, so accept and do nothing. Risk avoidance: eliminate the activity/device (benefits are lost too). Risk reduction: take action to reduce exposure/impact (most common). Risk transfer: transfer part/all of the risk to a third party such as an insurer.

Hacktivists: gray hat, protest political/social ideas (leaks, DDoS). Cybercriminals: black hat, for money. State-sponsored: steal government secrets, intelligence, sabotage. Describe white hat, black hat, and grey hat hackers.

White hat: ethical, finds vulnerabilities to protect networks, with permission. Black hat: unauthorized access for personal gain or harm. Grey hat: in between: finds/discloses vulnerabilities, sometimes without permission but without malicious intent (brokers, hacktivists).

Quiz 2021

Match: Phreaker, Spammer, Phisher, virus, worm, Trojan horse

Phisher: tricks others into giving sensitive info (credit card/passwords). Spammer: sends large quantities of unsolicited email. Phreaker: manipulates the phone network (free long-distance calls). Virus: malicious software attached to another program. Worm: executes arbitrary code and installs copies of itself in memory, infecting other hosts. 2.4 Reconnaissance, Access, and Social Engineering

  • Recon: information query (whois) ← ping sweep ← port scan ← vulnerability scanner ← exploitation tools.
  • Access: password، spoofing (IP/MAC/DHCP)، trust exploitation، port redirection، MiTM، buffer overflow.
  • Social engineering: pretexting، phishing، spear phishing، spam، something for something، baiting، impersonation، tailgating، shoulder surfing، dumpster diving. الحل: تدريب الموظفين (weakest link).

Explain: Reconnaissance, Access, and Denial of service attacks

Recon: information gathering and unauthorized mapping of systems/services/vulnerabilities (query, ping sweep, port scan, vulnerability scan, exploitation tools); precedes the other attacks. Access: exploits known vulnerabilities in authentication/FTP/web services to gain entry (password, spoofing, trust exploitation, port redirection, MiTM, buffer overflow). DoS: interrupts network services by overwhelming traffic or maliciously formatted packets (DDoS = multiple coordinated sources: zombies, botnet, handlers, botmaster).

Match the social engineering techniques with their descriptions

2.5 DoS, Buffer Overflows, and Evasion

  • DoS: overwhelming traffic أو malformed packets. DDoS: zombies، bots، botnet، handlers (C2)، botmaster. مثال Mirai.
  • Buffer overflow: إغراق الـ memory بقيم غير متوقعة (حوالي ثلث الهجمات).
  • Evasion: encryption/tunneling، resource exhaustion، traffic fragmentation، protocol-level misinterpretation، traffic substitution/insertion، pivoting، rootkits، proxies.
